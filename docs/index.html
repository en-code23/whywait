BUILT TO GET OUT OF YOUR WAY

Small app.
Proper little escape.

  1. Open. Pick. Play.

    Choose from the launcher or menu bar. Only one game is active at a time; your desktop stays visible.

  2. Back to work in a keypress.

    Escape returns to the launcher. Close it and WhyWait waits in the menu bar. Command-Q quits completely.

  3. Your catches come with you.

    FishDex, your collection, coins, rods, and equipment are saved locally on your Mac. No account, no cloud required.

A quick note before installing

Unzip the download and move WhyWait to Applications. This independent build is not Apple-notarized, so macOS may block the first launch.

Only if you trust this release: after attempting to open it, check System Settings → Privacy & Security for Open Anyway. Don’t disable Gatekeeper globally.
